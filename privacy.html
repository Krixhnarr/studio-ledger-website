Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · Applies to the Studio Ledger desktop app, the Android app and studioledger.in

Studio Ledger runs an architecture studio's projects, drawings, people, time and money. That means we hold information about your studio and your team, so we keep to a simple rule: we use it to run Studio Ledger for you, and for nothing else. We do not sell it, and we do not show advertising.

01Who we are

"Studio Ledger", "we" and "us" mean the people who run Studio Ledger and the website studioledger.in. There are two kinds of information in Studio Ledger, and we treat them differently:

  • Your account (your name, email and sign-in). We decide how this is used, so we are responsible for it.
  • Your studio's information (projects, drawings' details, attendance, messages and so on). The studio owns this. We only store and process it on the studio's behalf, to provide the service. The principal who created the studio decides who in the team can see what.

03Your files stay with you

Drawing files (PDFs and images), photographs and videos are not kept on our servers. They are kept in your studio's own shared office folder. The server only keeps a short description of each sheet, such as its title, number and where it stands in review.

When a team member is away from the office and sends a file from site, it goes into your firm's own Google Drive (if the firm has linked one), and a computer in your office then moves it into the office folder. Those files pass between your devices and Google; they do not pass through our servers.

04Google sign-in and Google Drive

Studio Ledger uses Google in two optional ways. Both are off unless you choose them.

Continue with Google

We ask Google for your name, your email address and a stable identifier for your Google account (the scopes openid, email and profile). We use these only to create your Studio Ledger account and sign you in. We never see your Google password.

The firm's Google Drive

A principal or the office admin can link the firm's Google account so that files added on site can reach the office. We ask only for the narrow permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, Studio Ledger can see and manage only the folder and files it creates itself. It cannot see, open, list or change anything else in that Google Drive.

  • Studio Ledger creates a folder, places files sent from site into it, and sends each file to the Drive's trash after the office has safely copied it.
  • The access token that allows this is stored encrypted on our server and is used only for these actions.
  • You can stop at any time: unlink the Drive in Settings → Google Drive for site work, or remove Studio Ledger at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Either one ends our access.
Google API Services User Data Policy. Studio Ledger's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide the sign-in and the file-transfer features you asked for. We do not sell it, do not use it for advertising, do not use it to train AI models, and no person at Studio Ledger reads it except where you ask us for support or the law requires it.

05How we use it

  • To run Studio Ledger: sign you in, show each person what their role allows, check drawings against your studio's standards, keep attendance and leave, and deliver messages and notifications.
  • To send you the emails the service needs: a sign-in or reset code, a welcome, and notices about your plan or invoices.
  • To take payment and keep the records the law requires.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and prevent misuse.
  • To improve Studio Ledger using counts and errors, not by reading your studio's contents.

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not read your studio's data except when you ask us to help, or to investigate misuse or a legal request.

07Claude, if you switch it on

Studio Ledger has optional features that use Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic: for example reading a drawing sheet against your studio's standards, or helping write a summary. These are off by default. A studio turns them on by adding its own Anthropic API key in Settings.

When you use one, the relevant content (for example the text and image of the sheet being reviewed, and your studio's standards) is sent to Anthropic to produce the answer, under the studio's own agreement with Anthropic. Nothing is sent unless someone uses the feature. You can remove the key at any time to stop it.

08How long we keep it

  • While your account or studio is active: we keep it so the service works.
  • When a studio is closed: a principal can close it. Its data is then archived and kept securely (not used, and not visible to your team) so it can be handed back to the studio if asked for. You can ask us to delete the archive permanently.
  • Payment and invoice records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law require.
  • Security logs are kept for a limited period and then deleted.
  • Backups are replaced on a rolling schedule, so deleted information leaves backups within a few weeks.

09How we protect it

  • Everything travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Google Drive access tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Money-related fields, such as rates and fees, are stored sealed and shown only to principals and to people a principal has given the finances power.
  • Access is by role: juniors and interns do not see what seniors and principals see, and personal notes and chats are visible only to the people in them.
  • Important actions are recorded in an audit trail.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell the affected studios and the authorities as the law requires.

10Your choices and rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and similar laws elsewhere, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your account or your studio's data (some records, such as invoices, we must keep);
  • stop a connection (Google, Instagram, a mailbox, Claude) at any time;
  • withdraw a consent you gave, and make a complaint, to us first and then to the Data Protection Board of India or the authority where you live.

A team member's request about information their studio holds is best made to the studio's principal first, because the studio is responsible for that information. We will help with any request. Write to us at the address in section 12.

11Children

Studio Ledger is a tool for professional practices and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think we have, tell us and we will delete it.

12Changes and contact
