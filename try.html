Your studio writes the rules, as MUST or SHOULD. The title block and file name are read straight from the PDF; AI review looks at the drawing itself. A sheet that fails a MUST rule goes back to its author with a fix list, and people still approve every sheet.

Move the sliders. The pay, the breakdown and the calendar update as you go.

This is the rule the Salary panel on the Finances page uses: monthly salary ÷ working days × days payable. Site, remote and late days are paid. Your studio decides whether overtime is paid and at what rate (1.5× here), what happens on a holiday worked, and whether unused leave carries over. Salaries are sealed: only the principal and people given the finances power can see them.
