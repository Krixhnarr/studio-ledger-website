Get Studio Ledger

The desktop app runs the whole studio on Windows. The Android app keeps principals and senior architects in touch when they’re away from their desk. Every new studio starts with a 30-day free trial, no card needed.

Version

Desktop app

Studio Ledger for Windows

For everyone in the studio: principals, architects, interns and the office admin.

System
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
Also needed
An internet connection, and a shared office folder for drawing files
Updates
Installed for you; Settings → Updates & help shows what changed
Download for Windows ↓
  1. Run the installer. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info → Run anyway.
  2. Create your account with your email (we send a six-digit code) or with Continue with Google.
  3. Principals choose Create a studio; everyone else chooses Join a studio and types the Studio ID.
Android

Phone app

Studio Ledger for Android

For principals, senior architects and the office admin. Juniors and interns use the desktop app.

What it does
See who’s in and who’s late, approve leave, return drawings, check tasks and the schedule, and reply to the team
Before you install
A principal switches it on in Settings → Files & devices → Phone app, for the studio or person by person
iPhone
Not available yet
Download for Android ↓
  1. Open this page on the phone and download the app, then open the file.
  2. Android asks once to allow installing apps from your browser. Allow it.
  3. Sign in with the same email and password you use on the computer.
