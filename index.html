02Drawing review

Your standards,
checked on every sheet.

When someone submits a PDF, Studio Ledger reads it the moment it lands. A sheet only reaches a senior once it meets the standards your studio wrote.

  1. SubmitAuthor uploads the PDF and ticks the visual self-check
  2. Automatic checkSheet number, scale, paper size, title block and file name read from the PDF
  3. Claude reviewOptional: judges the drawing itself and says where the problem is
  4. Senior reviewMarks each visual check OK or Flag, redlines the sheet
  5. PrincipalApproves or returns. Every step stays in the sheet's history

A failed MUST rule sends the sheet back to the author with a fix list, before a senior spends any time on it.

What it checks

  • From the file: sheet-number format, file naming, allowed scales, paper size, required title-block text, pages per file
  • From your checklist: line weights, dimensions, north point, section markers, hatches, revision clouds, anything your studio writes down
  • Pass mark: no MUST rule failed and a score at or above 80 (you set it)

With Claude connected

  • Claude looks at the drawing itself against your standards and principles, and says where on the sheet the problem is
  • Decides mode overrides a self-ticked box that isn't true. Advises mode only adds notes. A reviewer's OK or Flag always wins
  • Authors can pre-check a sheet before submitting. Spend stops at the monthly budget you set

Claude runs on your studio's own Anthropic key, so you pay Anthropic directly for what you use. Without internet, sheets fall back to the automatic checks.

    10Questions

    Questions studios ask.

    Does it need internet or a server?

    No special server. Studio Ledger keeps everything in a shared folder: a folder on a NAS, a shared drive on one PC, or a synced drive all work. It works offline; only Claude reviews and Ask Claude need internet.

    What do I need for the Claude drawing review?

    An Anthropic API key from console.anthropic.com, which the principal adds in Settings. You pay Anthropic directly for each check, and you set a monthly budget in Studio Ledger. A Claude Pro or Max chat subscription can't be used by other apps. Solo includes 10 Claude checks a month; paid plans have no cap.

    Which file types can be checked?

    PDF is recommended: paper size, sheet number, scale and title-block text are read straight from it. Export your sheets from AutoCAD, Revit, ArchiCAD or SketchUp Layout as PDF as you would for issue.

    What happens when the trial ends?

    Choose a plan and paste the licence key. If you don't, Studio Ledger moves to the free Solo plan; a studio with more people or projects than Solo allows becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted, and a licence key unlocks everything again.

    Do I pay per person?

    No. Each plan includes a number of people (5, 15 or 30), with a lower price for every extra person on larger plans. Use the calculator above to see your price.

    Does it work on Mac?

    Not yet. Studio Ledger runs on Windows 10 and 11.
