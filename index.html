01The problem

Architecture is complicated enough. Running your studio shouldn’t be.

A team, a process, deadlines, drawings, clients and fees. The design gets your full attention; everything around it ends up spread across chats, folders, spreadsheets and memory.

The day startswith chasing.

  • Chasing updates
  • Deadlines slipping
  • Reviews piling up
  • Information scattered
  • Mistakes repeating

Studio Ledger opens on one overview: what needs your attention, who’s waiting on whom, what’s due this week and who’s in. Every submitted sheet is checked against your standards, so repeated mistakes surface before review.

Next · The team ↓

Good people,left waiting.

  • Team performance unclear
  • Workloads unbalanced
  • Productivity unmeasured
  • Instructions forgotten
  • Team members waiting

Every instruction becomes a task with an owner and a date. Performance shows each person’s output, first-pass rate, current load and hours, so you can rebalance the work before someone burns out.

Next · Time and drawings ↓

Where didthe week go?

  • Time unaccounted for
  • Attendance untracked
  • Drawing revisions confused
  • Project status unclear
  • Meetings undocumented

Check-ins, leave and timesheets are recorded as the day happens. The drawing register keeps every sheet’s revision, scale and status for each project, transmittals record what was issued to whom, and meeting minutes are filed with the project.

Next · The wider studio ↓

Everything aroundthe drawings.

  • Contacts hard to find
  • Team locations unknown
  • Morale overlooked
  • Finances unorganised
  • Social media unmanaged

Fees, invoices and payments sit beside the work: what’s ready to invoice, what’s awaiting payment and how much of each fee is used against progress. Contacts, who’s on site or remote, team happiness and the studio’s Instagram live in the same place.

3.3 · Studio Standards

Write your drawing rules once. Check them on every sheet.

The principal sets the studio’s core principles and drawing rules: title blocks, sheet layout, line work, dimensions, annotation, symbols, CAD layers and file naming. Each rule is a MUST or a SHOULD, and a sheet passes when no MUST rule fails and its score reaches your pass mark.

Start from the built-in set or write your own. Some rules are checked automatically from the PDF; the rest go to the author’s checklist and the reviewer, or to Claude.

05How it works

Up and running in an afternoon.

Each person installs Studio Ledger on their own Windows PC and signs in with their own account. The studio’s records are kept on our server; your drawing files stay in a shared folder in the office.

  1. Step 01

    Install Studio Ledger

    Download the installer for Windows 10 or 11 and run it. Create your account with your email or with Google.

  2. Step 02

    Set up the studio, invite the team

    The principal creates the studio and chooses the drawing files folder. Everyone else joins with the Studio ID, and the principal approves each person.

  3. Step 03

    Run the practice from it

    Add projects and your standards, submit drawings, assign work. After 30 days, a principal chooses a plan and pays in the app.

The full setup guide

What each person does, in order. Nothing here needs an IT team.

A · Office server or NAS · once

Prepare the drawing files folder

  1. Create a shared folder every studio PC can reach, for example \\OFFICE-NAS\Studio. A file server, a NAS or an always-on office PC all work.
  2. Give each studio member read and write access to it.
  3. Turn on scheduled backups of the folder. Most NAS drives can do this for you. Drawing files live only here.

B · Principal’s PC · first

Create the studio

  1. Download the installer and run it. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info → Run anyway.
  2. Create your account with your email (we send a six-digit code) or with Continue with Google.
  3. Choose Create a studio, name it, pick the plan to try and choose the drawing files folder.
  4. Write your drawing rules in Standards, or start from the built-in set.
  5. Optional: connect AI review in Settings → Claude review with your Anthropic API key and a monthly budget.

C · Everyone else

Join the studio

  1. Install Studio Ledger and create your own account the same way.
  2. Choose Join a studio and type the Studio ID the principal gives you. It is shown in Team → Add person.
  3. The principal or office admin approves the request and picks your role.
  4. Choose the drawing files folder on your PC in Settings → Files & devices.

D · Principals, seniors, office admin · phone

The phone app

  1. A principal turns it on in Settings → Files & devices → Phone app, for the studio or person by person.
  2. Android: download the app on the phone and open it. Android asks once to allow installing apps from your browser; allow it.
  3. Sign in with the same email and password you use on the computer.
  4. See who’s in, approve leave, return drawings and reply to the team from anywhere.

Product

What is included in the 30-day trial?

Every feature for the whole team, with no card needed. After 30 days a principal chooses a plan; choose annual billing and you get two months free.

Is there a phone app?

Yes, for Android, for principals, senior architects and the office admin: who’s in and who’s late, leave and drawings waiting on you, tasks, the schedule and the studio’s chats, with approvals from the phone. Fees, happiness and complaints go only to principals’ phones. Juniors and interns use the desktop app. There is no iPhone app yet.

Does it work on Mac?

Not yet. Studio Ledger runs on Windows 10 and 11.

Installation

Does it need internet or a server?

It needs the internet: everyone signs in to the Studio Ledger server, which keeps the studio’s records. You don’t run a server yourself. You only need a shared folder in the office (a NAS or a shared drive) for the drawing files.

What do we need before we start?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) on every PC, an internet connection, and a shared folder for drawing files that every PC can open. The setup guide walks through each step.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is that expected?

Yes. When you run the installer, choose More info → Run anyway.

How do updates work?

Studio Ledger checks for a new version and installs it for you. Settings → Updates & help shows what changed.

AI drawing review

What do I need for AI drawing review?

An Anthropic API key from console.anthropic.com, which the principal adds in Settings → Claude review. You pay Anthropic directly for each check and set a monthly budget in Studio Ledger. A Claude Pro or Max chat subscription can’t be used by other apps. Solo includes 10 AI checks a month; paid plans have no cap.

Which file types can be checked?

PDF. Export your sheets from AutoCAD, Revit, ArchiCAD or SketchUp Layout as PDF, as you would for issue. Paper size, sheet number, scale and title-block text are read straight from it.

Does the AI approve drawings?

No. A failed MUST rule returns the sheet with a fix list, but seniors and the principal still have the final say on every sheet.

Pricing and licensing

What happens when the trial ends?

A principal chooses a plan in Settings → Plan & billing and pays in the app. If you don’t, the studio moves to the free Solo plan; a studio with more people or projects than Solo allows becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted, and buying a plan unlocks everything again. Each person gets one free trial.

Can we pay for 3 or 6 months?

Yes. Pay monthly, for 3 months (5% off), 6 months (10% off) or a year (two months free). A principal chooses in Settings → Plan & billing.

The first 10 studios to pay for a plan get 30% off every payment for their first 12 months, on any plan and any period. A place is counted when the payment goes through, not when you sign up or start the trial. It isn't available with automatic renewal.

Do I pay per person?

No. Each plan includes a number of people (5, 15 or 30), with a lower price for every extra person on larger plans.

Can a studio outside India use it?

Yes. Studios outside India pay in US dollars by card, with no GST: $69, $149 or $279 a month for Studio, Practice or Firm.

Is there a launch offer?
